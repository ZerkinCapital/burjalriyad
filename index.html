Teaching, work, residence and amenity, stacked on a single plot of Riyadh.

Burj Al Riyad is a proposed mixed-use tower. The scheme brings academic space, student housing, offices and serviced apartments into one building, with medical floors for diagnostics, rehabilitation, pharma and wellness below, dining in the middle, and a shared sky lobby with high dining at the top.
