One building, several institutions.

Universities entering the Gulf carry the cost of land and construction before a single student enrols. Burj Al Riyad removes that. The building is developed, owned and maintained by its landlord; institutions take space and run their programmes.

Shared fitness facility, food and beverage and residential floors serve every institution in the building, at a scale no single faculty could justify alone.
